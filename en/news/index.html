How the Intel Room grows

  1. 1You send a link. A public post, a video or a report about Iran that the news missed. One line on what it shows. Never a private chat, and never from inside Iran.
  2. 2I check it. That it opens, that it shows what it says, where and when. Usually within a day.
  3. 3It goes on the map with your name. Anyone can find it, Kaveh and the other shows can put it on air, and you get the XP.

See the answers in the team room

Nothing is hosted here: every item opens where it was first posted, except the regime's media, which is marked and never linked. Every hour the room reads Persian and English outlets, their YouTube channels and Persian X. Every source carries its camp from a register that says why: who owns it, runs it or pays for it, and whether that is public record or reported (the Media tab). A source not rated yet says so, and one that points at Iran is treated as the regime's until it is rated. "Hot" is measured from how many outlets carry a story, how fast a video is watched or a post is liked, never guessed. A place on the map comes from the news itself, or from a reader's find once it is checked.
