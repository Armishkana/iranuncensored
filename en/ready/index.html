How to send them

Send the pictures first: they pass through Telegram, WhatsApp and Bluetooth easily, and each one carries the three most important things to do. Then the PDFs, which work with no internet once they are on the phone. The address of this section is iranuncensored.com/fa/ready/; nothing on it asks a reader for anything, and nothing a reader taps is sent anywhere.

Pictures travel when PDFs do not. Send them by Telegram, WhatsApp or Bluetooth.

Your safety with this page

  • What this page keeps stays in this browser: ticks, drill answers and the family card. Nothing is sent to any server, and by default it is gone when the visit ends.
  • If the phone could be searched, fill the family card with a nickname and keep the PDFs in a hidden or locked folder.
  • Quick exit: one tap clears everything in this section and opens an ordinary page. On a computer, Esc twice does the same.
  • Private (incognito) mode keeps nothing, but it cannot save an offline copy either. Choose one: private, or saved.
  • An offline copy means the pages and PDFs stay in the browser. Anyone who opens this phone and this address can see them.
