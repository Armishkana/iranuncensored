  • A short video first: 1 to 3 minutes of you talking, in English for the English panel, in Persian for the Persian show. The form says what to cover.
  • For the English panel, English good enough to discuss politics live.
  • A decent microphone and a stable connection, and a camera for the English panel.
  • No account: the form takes your name, your email and the link to your video.
  • On the English panel, your camera on for the whole show, with your own face. On the Persian show, your voice alone is fine.
  • That you show up, follow the show and let other people speak. It's a discussion.
  • Any politics. The team isn't meant to be an echo chamber.
