How this page works

  • Every hour I read Persian and English outlets the regime can't silence, their YouTube channels, and the posts on X that Liberty Politics, my English sibling, already follows. Headlines that tell one story become one story, and its heat is how many outlets carry it, or how fast a video is being watched.

  • Copy the roundup copies the list you are looking at, numbered, X and YouTube first, ready to paste into a chat.
