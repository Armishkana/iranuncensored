    Rally

    In person, outside Iran only: a dorehami of people you already trust, or a public event from the board.

    Public events outside IranHow to host a dorehamiSafety, inside Iran and outside

    • Host your first dorehamiAn evening300 XPOutside Iran only

      Three to five people you trust, food, one question. It happens because you asked.

      Why it matters. A free Iran starts with people who trust each other enough to talk. A dorehami builds that, one table at a time.

      1. Invite three to five people you already trust. No public post: message each one yourself.
      2. Put food on the table and one question: what would you do in the first week of a free Iran?
      3. Agree the rule before anyone takes a picture: the table, the flag, hands.
      4. Set the date of the next one before people leave.

      The rules of this one

      • Only people you knew before this site. Someone who says they are a member here is still a stranger.
      • I keep no list of dorehamis, I match no strangers, and I never send an invitation or a file. If someone says I sent them, I did not.
      • Pictures show the table, the flag, hands. A person is shown only if they said yes to this site and the shows too, not just to your own post.
      • Nobody whose family is in Iran is shown or named, unless they chose it themselves.
      • No address, no house number and no car plate, on this site or in any picture.
      • Nothing is posted during it, and nobody says where it was, before or after.
      • A dorehami belongs to its host. I give a guide and XP. I run nothing, pay for nothing and decide nothing.

      What to send as proof. How many came, what did you eat, and what did the table disagree on?

      No names, no faces, no address. A report is a public post.

    • Change one mindAn evening150 XP

      A relative or a friend who still believes the regime, or isn't sure. One real talk.

      Why it matters. The regime lives on doubt as much as on fear. A mind changed abroad often reaches a family inside.

      1. Pick one person you love who still defends the Islamic Republic, or shrugs.
      2. Listen first. Ask what they are afraid of if it falls.
      3. Bring one fact, not ten. A name, a date, a number.
      4. Leave the door open. Minds move after the talk, not during it.

      What to send as proof. What moved them, even a little? No name.

      No name and nothing that shows who they are.

      Why it matters. For 47 years the world has heard the regime's version. One minute from a real Iranian answers it.

      1. Film one minute, upright, in Persian, English or the language where you live.
      2. You never have to show your face. Hands, a voice and a flag are enough.
      3. Post it on your own account and send me the link.

      What to send as proof. The link to your post.

      Nobody whose family is in Iran is shown, unless they chose it themselves.

    How XP works

    Only an accepted report earns XP. I read every report, and no photos are ever needed. If one is too thin I send it back and say why, and you can report again.

    Making your account, your first post and your first reply tick themselves.

    A public event posted on the board is proven by the people who came: each one rates it, and the organiser's XP follows their ratings. A dorehami of people you already trust is a short report in your own words, with no names.

    Hosting a gathering earns more than coming to one, and coming earns more than anything online.

    XP is a count of what you did. It is worth nothing in cash and buys nothing.

    Outside the missions I give a little XP by hand, for quality and never for how much someone posts, and I write the reason beside it.
