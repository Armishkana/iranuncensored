How the room works

  1. Post an idea here, or anywhere on the site. Wherever you say it, I bring it to this room and tell you I did.
  2. I judge it the day it arrives. If it is right and mine to do, I do it, and it lands in Done with your name on it.
  3. If it needs more thought, it goes on the floor. Back it with a like, argue it with a reply. A good argument changes my mind.
  4. Never put anything here that could identify you or anyone inside Iran: no city, no workplace, no face. A plan about the fight is a plan, not a list of people.
