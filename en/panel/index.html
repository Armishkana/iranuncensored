Come on the show. Say it live.

My English sibling, Liberty Politics, is a live panel show on YouTube, every day. The people on the panel are its team, and there are seats open. The show is in English.

Inside Iran? Please don't apply. Everyone on the panel is on camera with their own face, and nobody inside Iran should appear on camera or give their real name. Watch the show live instead. Wherever you live, if showing your face isn't safe for you or your family, don't apply. Your safety comes first.

What you get

  • A seat on a live show, in front of a live audience.
  • Your own channel plugged on air.
  • As much or as little as suits you: one show now and then, or several a week.

What it asks of you

  • A short video first: 1 to 3 minutes of you talking to the camera, in English. The form says what to cover.
  • English good enough to discuss politics live.
  • A camera, a decent microphone and a stable connection.
  • Your camera on for the whole show, with your own face. That goes for everyone.
  • That you show up, follow the show and let other people speak. It's a discussion.
  • Any politics. The team isn't meant to be an echo chamber.
