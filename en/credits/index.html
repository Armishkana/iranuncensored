The pictures

Every picture on this site was drawn with AI for this site, by one hand, from the stories of the Shahnameh and the lives of Iranians today. Nobody real is drawn in them, and none of them carries words.

  • Mount Damavand at dusk with a chained giant in its cave. Drawn with AI for this site.
  • Kaveh the blacksmith raises his leather apron as a banner over a crowd. Drawn with AI for this site.
  • Persepolis ruins at night with a lone hooded figure. Drawn with AI for this site.
  • The Simurgh over the mountains at dawn. Drawn with AI for this site.
  • Fereydun with the ox-headed mace faces Zahhak on his throne. Drawn with AI for this site.

The drawings

The six rank marks and the small drawings on the missions were drawn for this site. The ranks follow Kaveh's banner: in the Shahnameh, a blacksmith's leather apron tied to a spear.
