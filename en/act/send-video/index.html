What we are looking for

  • What is happening where you are. A protest, a strike, a crackdown, a slogan on a wall, a queue, an empty shelf. Anything the Islamic Republic would rather nobody filmed.
  • Trump, meet Pahlavi. Write the three words on whatever you have, film them, and send the video. 16 are up so far. See the campaign
  • Pahlavi Speaks. Your best short edit of Prince Reza Pahlavi's own words, for people who have never heard him. See the brief

Two ways to send it

  1. 1

    Send the video itself on Telegram

    Send it to Armin Navabi, the host of Iran Uncensored. Telegram takes large files and works through a VPN. Add one line: where it was filmed and when, if that is safe to say.

  2. 2

    Already posted it? Send the link

If you are inside Iran

  • We never publish who sent a video. No name, no username, no city unless you tell us to.
  • We look at every video before it is used. One that could show who filmed it is left out.
  • If a face or a voice in it should not be seen or heard, say so in your message.
  • Use a VPN, and send from an account that does not carry your real name or number.
  • After it is sent, delete the chat on your side. We already have the file.

What happens to it

The videos that hold up are shown on the live show, posted on X with IranUncensored.com on them, or added to this site. We cannot use every video, and we cannot answer every message.
