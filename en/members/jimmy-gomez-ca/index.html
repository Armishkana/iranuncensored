  • With

    Cosponsored: Enhanced Iran Sanctions Act of 2025

    Sanctions any foreign company, port, refinery or ship that helps process, export or sell Iranian oil and gas. It goes after the oil money that keeps the regime alive.

  • With

    Voted Yea: Condemning Iran's drone and missile attack on Israel (H.Res. 1143)

    Condemns the April 2024 Iranian drone and missile attack on Israel and backs Israel's right to defend itself. It stands against the regime's direct attack on a US ally.

    18 April 2024 · the official record

  • Against

    Voted Nay: Standing Against Houthi Aggression Act (H.R. 6046)

    Requires the Houthis, the Iran-backed force in Yemen, to be listed as a foreign terrorist organization and sanctioned. It puts maximum pressure on one of the regime's main proxy forces.

    17 April 2024 · the official record

  • Against

    Voted Nay: Iran Sanctions Relief Review Act (H.R. 4691)

    Lets Congress review and block any move by a president to lift or waive sanctions on Iran. It makes it harder for any administration to give the regime sanctions relief.

    17 April 2024 · the official record

  • Against

    Voted Nay: Rescinding Iran sanctions waivers (H.R. 5947)

    Cancels the waivers that let Iran reach about 6 billion dollars held abroad and bars new waivers of that kind. It undoes sanctions relief given to the regime.

    17 April 2024 · the official record

  • Against

    Voted Nay: No U.S. Financing for Iran Act (H.R. 5921)

    Bars the Treasury from licensing US financial dealings tied to trade with Iran, apart from humanitarian goods, and tells US representatives to oppose IMF help for Iran. It cuts the regime off from US finance and international bailouts.

    15 April 2024 · the official record

  • Against

    Voted Nay: No Funds for Iranian Terrorism Act (H.R. 5961) key vote, counts double

    Freezes the roughly 6 billion dollars of Iranian money that was moved to Qatar under the 2023 prisoner deal by sanctioning any bank that helps Iran use it. It keeps money out of the regime's hands and reverses a sanctions-relief deal.

    30 November 2023 · the official record

  • With

    Voted Yea: A nuclear Islamic Republic is not acceptable (H.Res. 559)

    Declares it US policy that a nuclear-armed Islamic Republic of Iran is not acceptable, that all necessary means should be used to stop it, and that allies including Israel are free to act to prevent it. It commits the United States to stopping the regime's nuclear program by any means.

    1 November 2023 · the official record

  • Against

    Voted Nay: Ogles amendment to H.R. 4665: no funds to take the IRGC off the terrorist list

    Amendment to the 2024 State Department spending bill that bars any money from being used to remove the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps from the list of foreign terrorist organizations. It locks in the terrorist designation of the regime's main armed force.

    28 September 2023 · the official record

  • With

    Voted Yea: Stop Iranian Drones Act (H.R. 6089)

    Makes clear that US sanctions law covers anyone who supplies, sells or transfers combat drones to or from Iran. It tightens sanctions on the regime's drone program.

    27 April 2022 · the official record

Stands with the Iranian people

100out of 100

Backs the people in the streets: sanctions on those who kill them, an open internet when the regime cuts it, and Congress on record on their side.

  • With

    Voted Yea: MAHSA Act (H.R. 589) key vote, counts double

    Requires the President to decide whether to sanction the Supreme Leader, the President of Iran and their offices for human rights abuses and support for terrorism. It punishes the regime's top leaders for what they do to the Iranian people.

    12 September 2023 · the official record

  • With

    Voted Yea: Condemning the persecution of Iran's Baha'i minority (H.Res. 492)

    Condemns the Iranian government's state-sponsored persecution of the Baha'i religious minority and calls for sanctions on the officials responsible. It stands with Iranians persecuted by the regime for their beliefs.

    12 September 2023 · the official record

  • With

    Voted Yea: Commending the bravery of the Iranian protesters (H.Con.Res. 7) key vote, counts double

    Commends the women and men of Iran protesting in more than 133 cities after the death of Mahsa Amini and condemns the regime's violent crackdown. It puts Congress on the side of the Iranian people against the regime.

    25 January 2023 · the official record
