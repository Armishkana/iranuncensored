Get on the panel

My English sibling, Liberty Politics, is a live panel show on YouTube, on most days. There's room on the panel, and you can apply for a seat.

It covers U.S. politics, Israel and Iran, the rest of the Middle East, and world affairs like Ukraine, Europe and China. The show is in English.

Apply with a short video

About ten minutes. The form is in English. The seat is unpaid.

What you get

  • A seat on a live show, in front of a live audience.
  • Your own channel plugged on air.
  • As much or as little as suits you: one show now and then, or several a week.

What it asks of you

  • English good enough to discuss politics live. The show and the form are both in English.
  • A camera, a decent microphone and a stable connection.
  • Your camera on for the whole show, with your own face. That goes for everyone. If showing your face isn't safe for you or your family, don't apply. Your safety comes first.
  • That you show up, follow the show and let other people speak. It's a discussion.
  • Any politics. The panel isn't meant to be an echo chamber.
  • A short video first: one to three minutes of you talking to camera. The form tells you what to say and how to send it.

What happens after you apply

  • Armin Navabi, one of the hosts of the show, watches every video.
  • The people who fit get an email back.
  • The video comes first for everyone, so please don't ask for a call before it. Sending one doesn't guarantee a seat.
