Pahlavi Speaks

Most Americans have never heard Prince Reza Pahlavi speak for one full minute. Cut his strongest minute into a short vertical video, and we will put it in front of them.

Why one minute matters

Of the 539 members of Congress, only 9 are on record about him. The people who decide what America does about Iran have barely heard the man Iranians are asking for.

Nobody watches a forty minute speech. Everybody watches forty seconds, if the first sentence lands.

The brief

  • His own words, in English, from a real speech or interview. Never an AI voice, never a sentence he did not say.
  • Vertical, 9:16, sixty seconds or less.
  • Start on his strongest sentence. Cut the greeting and the introduction.
  • English subtitles on the picture. Most people watch with the sound off.
  • One idea per video. If he makes two points, make two videos.
  • Music and extra footage are welcome, if you have the right to use them.
  • Say in your message where the speech is from, so we can check the words against it.

Where to find his speeches

His own accounts carry the full recordings: Reza Pahlavi on X, Reza Pahlavi on YouTube.
