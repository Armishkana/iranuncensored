How the Intel Room grows

  1. 1You send a link. A public post, a video or a report about Iran that the news missed. One line on what it shows. Never a private chat, and never from inside Iran.

  3. 3It goes on the map with your name. Anyone can find it, Kaveh and the other shows can put it on air, and you get the XP.
