Who in Congress stands with Iran?

All 539 members of the United States Congress, graded on four things: Prince Reza Pahlavi, the regime, the Iranian people, and the MEK.

Behind every grade is the vote or the source. Open any name and check it yourself.

    Or see all 539 grades

    What the record shows

    • 118members earn an A.
    • 188fail.
    • 338are tied to the MEK: they signed one of its resolutions or spoke at its events. 161 have three ties or more.
    • 3are on record for Prince Reza Pahlavi. 6 are on record against him. The other 530 have said nothing we could find. Seen something? Send it.

    Four things we grade

    1. Stands with Prince Reza Pahlavi

      Has met him, backed him in public, or asked the White House to work with him. A member who says Iranians have rejected the monarchy is marked against.

      32 sourced statements

    2. Wants the Islamic Republic gone

      Votes for sanctions and pressure, against any deal that keeps the regime alive, and against pulling American forces out of the fight.

      31 votes and bills

    3. Stands with the Iranian people

      Backs the people in the streets: sanctions on those who kill them, an open internet when the regime cuts it, and Congress on record on their side.

      10 votes and bills

    4. No ties to the MEK

      The MEK is an exile group led by Maryam Rajavi that fought beside Saddam Hussein against Iran. Signing one of its resolutions in Congress, or speaking at its events, is a tie.

      6 votes and bills · 97 sourced statements
