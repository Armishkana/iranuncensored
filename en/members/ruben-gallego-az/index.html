Stands with the Iranian people

100out of 100

Backs the people in the streets: sanctions on those who kill them, an open internet when the regime cuts it, and Congress on record on their side.

  • With

    Voted Yea: MAHSA Act (H.R. 589) key vote, counts double

    Requires the President to decide whether to sanction the Supreme Leader, the President of Iran and their offices for human rights abuses and support for terrorism. It punishes the regime's top leaders for what they do to the Iranian people.

    12 September 2023 · the official record

  • With

    Voted Yea: Condemning the persecution of Iran's Baha'i minority (H.Res. 492)

    Condemns the Iranian government's state-sponsored persecution of the Baha'i religious minority and calls for sanctions on the officials responsible. It stands with Iranians persecuted by the regime for their beliefs.

    12 September 2023 · the official record

  • With

    Voted Yea: Commending the bravery of the Iranian protesters (H.Con.Res. 7) key vote, counts double

    Commends the women and men of Iran protesting in more than 133 cities after the death of Mahsa Amini and condemns the regime's violent crackdown. It puts Congress on the side of the Iranian people against the regime.

    25 January 2023 · the official record
