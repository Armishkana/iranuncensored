Run the screen for a live show

My sister, Liberty Politics, runs a daily live panel show on YouTube. It's hiring one person to run the screen, from home. The panel does the talking. You run the show.

  • Pay: 500 USD a month, after a two week paid trial at 125 USD a week.
  • Hours: about 5 hours a day, six days a week, two shows a day.
  • Show times: 11am to 2pm and 3pm to 5pm, U.S. Eastern time.
  • Where: from home.
  • Language: the work and the form are in English.
Apply with two short videos

What you'd do

  • Line up the news posts before the show, from a written list of rules.
  • Share your screen and change the post when it's time to move on.
  • Put up the banners, the Super Chat goal and the polls.
  • Show viewer comments on screen and make sure Super Chats get read.
  • Let guests in and check their camera and microphone.
  • Keep the show on time.

It all runs in StreamYard, in a browser. Full training is provided.

What it takes

  • Being reliable at the same hours every day.
  • Being calm and quick with a browser, tabs and a live chat.
  • Good written and spoken English.
  • Following the news, or wanting to.
  • Stable internet and a quiet place to work.

You don't give political opinions on air. On some days you may be on camera in a corner of the screen. The form asks for your name and a video of you. If that isn't safe where you live, don't apply.

How to apply

  • Fill in the form. It asks for two short videos: about 2 minutes of your screen while you run a test broadcast in StreamYard (a free account is enough), and 30 seconds of you on camera saying hello. Applications without the videos aren't read.
  • Armin Navabi, one of the hosts of the show, watches every video.
  • The people who fit get an email back. The videos come first for everyone, so please don't ask for a call before them.
  • A yes starts the two week paid trial.
