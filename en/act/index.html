My missions

    Get Washington on the record for Iran. Only 9 of 539 members of Congress have said where they stand on Prince Reza Pahlavi. Pick a mission, do it, then come back and show what you got.

    Main quest: Get them on the record

    Four stages. Each one is a mission of its own, and each is worth more than the last.

    0 of 4 done

    • 1Find your member's Iran score5 minutes50 XP

      Two senators and one representative work for you. See what each has done about Iran.

      Only for: US citizen, US resident.

      Why. Of 539 members of Congress, 9 are on record about Prince Reza Pahlavi. Mostly, nobody who votes for them has asked.

      1. Type your ZIP code on house.gov and write down your representative's name.
      2. Search that name on the scorecard. Then pick your state to find your two senators.
      3. On each page, read the line about Prince Reza Pahlavi: for him, against him, or no record yet.

      What to send as proof. Which three names, and the score beside each?

      • Find your representative on house.gov
      • Open the scorecard
    • 2Ask their office where they standAn hour150 XP

      Mostly, nobody who votes for them has asked. Be the one who does. Ask for a yes or a no.

      Only for: US citizen, US resident.

      Why. Asked what moves a member of Congress who has not made up their mind, 94 in 100 congressional staff said a visit in person from people who live in the district. Source: Congressional Management Foundation, 2017

      1. Call the district office, or use the contact form on the member's own site. Say you live in the district.
      2. Ask: will you say in public that the President should meet Prince Reza Pahlavi? Yes or no.
      3. Then ask: do you want the Islamic Republic gone, and will you stay away from the MEK?
      4. Write down the day, the office and the answer, word for word if you can.

      What to send as proof. Which office, which day, and what did they answer?

      No staff member's name, and no recording of a phone call. Write down what was said.

      • Find the office on house.gov
      • Every senator's contact page
      • The three questions, in the guide
    • 3Get the answer in publicEvery week300 XP

      An answer on the phone can be taken back. A letter, a post or a video cannot.

      Only for: US citizen, US resident.

      Why. What a member says in public goes on their page here and into their score. What is said in private cannot.

      1. Ask the office to put its answer in a letter or an email addressed to you.
      2. Or go to a public town hall with two or three neighbours and ask the member on camera.
      3. A signed letter, a public post, or a video of the member answering: each one counts.

      What to send as proof. The link to the post or the video, or to a photo of the letter

      Cover your home address on the letter. No other person's face without their yes.

      • Step five of the guide: ask on the record

      A sourced answer moves their score, for everyone who looks them up after you.

      Only for: US citizen, US resident.

      Why. 530 members of Congress have said nothing I could find about Prince Reza Pahlavi. Every answer you bring takes one off that number.

      1. Open the member's page from the scorecard and switch to its discussion.
      2. Choose A source, paste the link, and quote the exact sentence the member said.
      3. I check the quote against the link. What holds up goes on their page and into their score.

    • Cut one minute of Pahlavi SpeaksAn hour300 XP

      Most Americans have never heard Prince Reza Pahlavi speak for one full minute.

      Why. Nobody watches a forty minute speech. Everybody watches forty seconds, if the first sentence lands.

      1. Pick a real speech or interview of his in English. Never an AI voice, never a sentence he did not say.
      2. Cut it vertical, 9:16, sixty seconds or less. Start on his strongest sentence.
      3. Put English subtitles on the picture. Most people watch with the sound off.
      4. Post it with #PahlaviSpeaks and bring the link, with the name of the speech.

      What to send as proof. The link to your edit, and which speech it is cut from

      His real words only. An edit with one line he did not say is refused.

      • The brief, and where his speeches are
    • Subtitle a Persian speech in EnglishAn hour250 XP

      What Prince Reza Pahlavi tells Iranians in Persian, most Americans have never read.

      Why. The people who decide what America does about Iran have barely heard the man Iranians are asking for.

      1. Pick one minute of a Persian speech from his own accounts.
      2. Translate it into plain English, sentence by sentence. Add nothing and soften nothing.
      3. Put the English on the picture as subtitles, vertical if you can.
      4. Post it and bring the link, with the speech it is from, so I can check the translation.

      What to send as proof. The link to your video, and which speech it is from

      Never a word he did not say. I check the English against the Persian before it counts.

      Only an accepted report earns XP. I read every report. When the proof does not hold up I send it back and say why, and you can report again.

      Missions earn the most. Outside the missions I give a little XP by hand, for quality and never for how much someone posts, and I write the reason beside it.

      Nobody inside Iran is asked to report, so nothing here earns XP from inside Iran.
