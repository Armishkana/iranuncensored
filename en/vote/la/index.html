How to vote in Louisiana

You're a U.S. citizen? Then you vote like anyone else. Five questions, in the order you'll ask them. Every date links to the page I read it on.

Can I still register?

  • Online: by Tuesday 13 October.
  • By mail: your form has to be postmarked by Monday 5 October.
  • In person: by Monday 5 October.

Same day registration: For this one, go by the state's own page: Louisiana Secretary of State, Elections and Voting.

Register on the state's own form. Not sure you are registered? Look yourself up on the state's own lookup.

Source: Louisiana Secretary of State, Elections and Voting. Checked 12 October 2026.

Can I vote early?

Yes. Early voting in person starts Tuesday 20 October.

Its last day: For this one, go by the state's own page: Louisiana Secretary of State, Elections and Voting.

Open 8:30 a.m. to 6 p.m., not on Sunday 25 October.

Source: Louisiana Secretary of State, Elections and Voting. Checked 12 October 2026.

Can I vote by mail?

Only with a reason the state accepts. Louisiana lists the reasons on its own page.

  • Ask for your ballot: your request has to arrive by Friday 30 October.
  • Send it back: your ballot has to arrive by 4:30 p.m. on Monday 2 November.

Ask for your ballot on the state's own form.

Source: Louisiana Secretary of State, Elections and Voting. Checked 12 October 2026.

What do I bring?

Bring a photo ID with your signature, or a U.S. military ID with your name and picture. Without a photo ID you can still vote by signing an affidavit after answering questions that identify you.

Source: Louisiana Secretary of State, Elections and Voting. Checked 12 October 2026.

Where and when on 3 November?

Election Day is Tuesday 3 November.

  • Polls are open: 6:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
  • Where you vote: find your polling place on the state's own lookup.

Source: Louisiana Secretary of State, Elections and Voting. Checked 12 October 2026.

The last word

The state's election office has it, not me. If anything here differs from Louisiana Secretary of State, Elections and Voting, the state is right. I checked these pages on 12 October 2026, and rules can change after that.
