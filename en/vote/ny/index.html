How to vote in New York

You're a U.S. citizen? Then you vote like anyone else. Five questions, in the order you'll ask them. Every date links to the page I read it on.

Can I still register?

  • Online: For this one, go by the state's own page: New York State Board of Elections.
  • By mail: your form has to arrive by Saturday 24 October.
  • In person: by Saturday 24 October.

Same day registration: For this one, go by the state's own page: New York State Board of Elections.

Register on the state's own form.

Source: New York State Board of Elections. Checked 12 October 2026.

Can I vote early?

Yes. Early voting in person runs from Saturday 24 October to Sunday 1 November.

Source: New York State Board of Elections. Checked 12 October 2026.

Can I vote by mail?

Yes. Any registered voter in New York can vote by mail, and nobody asks you why.

  • Ask for your ballot: your request has to arrive by Saturday 24 October.
  • Send it back: your ballot has to be postmarked by Tuesday 3 November.

Where the source gives a rule and not a day, I counted the day from it. In its words: “Applications requesting to receive an early mail ballot must be received by the board of elections in your county no later than ten days before the election.”

Source: New York State Board of Elections. Checked 12 October 2026.

What do I bring?

Most voters do not show ID. If your identity could not be checked when you registered, you are asked for ID the first time you vote.

Source: New York State Board of Elections. Checked 12 October 2026.

Where and when on 3 November?

Election Day is Tuesday 3 November.

  • Polls are open: 6:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m.
  • Where you vote: find your polling place through New York State Board of Elections.

Source: New York Election Law 8-100. Checked 12 October 2026.

The last word

The state's election office has it, not me. If anything here differs from New York State Board of Elections, the state is right. I checked these pages on 12 October 2026, and rules can change after that.
