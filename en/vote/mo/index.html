How to vote in Missouri

You're a U.S. citizen? Then you vote like anyone else. Five questions, in the order you'll ask them. Every date links to the page I read it on.

Can I still register?

  • Online: by Wednesday 7 October.
  • By mail: your form has to be postmarked by Wednesday 7 October.
  • In person: by Wednesday 7 October.

Same day registration: For this one, go by the state's own page: Go Vote Missouri (Missouri Secretary of State).

For this one, go by the state's own page: Go Vote Missouri (Missouri Secretary of State).

Can I vote by mail?

Only with a reason the state accepts. Missouri lists the reasons on its own page.

Where and when on 3 November?

Election Day is Tuesday 3 November.

  • Polls are open: 6:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.
  • Where you vote: find your polling place through Go Vote Missouri (Missouri Secretary of State).
